Practice only

All characters and scenarios are fictional. Feedback is generated by simple local wording checks, not an AI model, human coach, validated assessment, or prediction. Do not use it to assess employees, make employment decisions, or replace professional guidance. Follow your organization's policies and seek appropriate support for harassment, discrimination, threats, or formal disciplinary matters.

Your information

Use invented names and details. Do not enter confidential information or another person's private information. You control local exports and deletion. Keep copies somewhere private; imports and cloud recovery are not available.

Free practice and Plus

The three foundation scenarios, wording feedback, journal, and export are free. Steady Plus unlocks three additional stretch scenarios as a one-time, non-renewing in-app purchase in configured Android builds. The store provides the localized price and confirms payment before access is granted. There is no advertised free trial in this version.

Restore a purchase using the same Google Play account. Availability and refunds are subject to Google Play's terms and policies. A refunded or revoked purchase may remove Plus access, but previously saved journal entries remain available. Do not repurchase solely to resolve a restore error; contact the store using your purchase receipt.

Availability

Practice content is bundled into the Android app. Store actions require a connection. The browser is a free preview, not an app-store release. The app is provided as a practice tool without a guarantee of workplace outcomes or uninterrupted operation.
