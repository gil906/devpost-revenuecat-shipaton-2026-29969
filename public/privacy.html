Steady privacy policy. Updated September 15, 2026.

Local practice data

Steady stores your rehearsal text, fictional scenario choices, timestamps, readiness ratings, and unfinished draft in local storage on your device. It keeps the latest 100 completed sessions and one draft. The app does not upload these records, create a user account, send them to an AI service, or run analytics or advertising. Export copies this data to a file or clipboard at your request.

Purchases

On a configured Android build, the RevenueCat SDK creates an anonymous app user identifier and communicates purchase status, transaction and product information, and technical device/app information to RevenueCat and Google Play. No rehearsal text or readiness ratings are passed to the SDK. Purchases and restoring access need network connectivity. The browser preview does not initialize the SDK. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy for their processing and retention practices.

Web hosting

The browser sends normal requests for app files. Steady's included server stores no request logs or application records. A hosting provider or reverse proxy may process normal connection metadata such as your IP address under its own policy.

Security and retention

Steady does not encrypt local storage itself. Your unlocked device, browser extensions, or browser profile may expose your words. Use fictional details and avoid confidential work information. Deleting browser data or uninstalling the app can erase your records. There is no cloud backup or cross-device sync.

Your controls

Use My journal to export your data or permanently delete local practice data and drafts. This does not delete RevenueCat or store transaction records or remove a purchase. For transaction-related requests, use the purchase receipt or the store's support tools. No transcript is available to the app developer for recovery.

Scope

Steady is an English-language workplace practice aid intended for adults. It is not a professional assessment, HR service, or legal advice. It does not request access to contacts, microphone, camera, or location.
